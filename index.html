🤔 What's the Difference?

Students always confuse these three temperatures. Let's meet the thermometer family!

Meet The Thermometer Family!

🌡️😊

Dry Bulb (DB)

30°C

"I measure the ACTUAL air temperature!"

• Regular thermometer

• Not affected by humidity

• What you see on weather apps

🌡️💦

Wet Bulb (WB)

22°C

"I'm cooled by evaporation!"

• Thermometer with wet cloth

• Water evaporates → cools me down

• Always ≤ Dry Bulb

• Cooling towers can't cool below me!

🌡️❄️💧

Dew Point (DP)

18°C

"I'm where condensation starts!"

• Temperature for 100% RH

• Cool air to this temp → water droplets!

• Always ≤ Wet Bulb ≤ Dry Bulb

• Surfaces colder than me get wet!

The Golden Rule:

Dew Point ≤ Wet Bulb ≤ Dry Bulb

At 100% RH:

All three are EQUAL!

At low RH:

Big gap between them

Wet Bulb Depression:

DB - WB = 8°C

💡 Why Wet Bulb Matters:

  • Evaporative coolers can't cool below WB
  • Cooling towers limited by WB
  • Human comfort in humid heat (heat index)
  • WB > 35°C is deadly even in shade!

💡 Why Dew Point Matters:

  • Condensation on cold surfaces (windows, pipes)
  • AC dehumidification (cool below DP)
  • Mold risk in buildings
  • DP > 20°C feels muggy/uncomfortable
